Core Departmental Workflows

  • HR & Payroll: Tracks employee attendance, automatically calculates taxes, integrates with PMS for performance, and posts salary vouchers directly to Finance.
  • Procurement: Departments generate Purchase Requisitions (PR). Once approved, Procurement converts them to POs. Supply Chain handles vendor delivery.
  • Inventory: Goods Receipt Notes (GRN) automatically increase stock and trigger Accounts Payable for vendor invoicing.
  • Production & PMS: Consumes raw inventory based on Bill of Materials (BOM), logs QA checks, and transfers finished goods to sales inventory.
  • Asset Management: Tracks IT and lab equipment lifecycle, calculates monthly depreciation, and posts journal entries to Finance.
  • Finance & Accounts: The central nervous system. Automatically aggregates all AP/AR, payroll, and sales data into a unified General Ledger for real-time P&L reporting.
