Purchase Management Workflow

PR → Inventory → Finance → Approval → Procurement → PO → GRN → Accounts → Closure

1. Product Request (PR)

  • User creates a Product Request.
  • System validates the request requirements.
  • Invalid PRs are sent back for correction. Valid PRs move forward.

2. Inventory Check

  • System checks local stock availability.
  • If stock is available, items are issued directly.
  • If out of stock, PR is forwarded to the procurement queue.

3. Finance Approval

  • Budget availability is verified by finance.
  • Management approves based on financial thresholds.

4. Purchase Order (PO)

  • Supplier is selected (via RFQ or direct selection).
  • Purchase Order is generated and dispatched to the supplier.

5. Goods Receipt Note (GRN)

  • Goods arrive and undergo quality control.
  • GRN is generated, updating inventory stock instantly.

6. Invoicing & Payment

  • Supplier invoice is matched against PO and GRN (3-way matching).
  • Accounts payable clears the payment and the cycle closes.
